The crossy road I created has most of the similar traits to the normal Crossy Road App. It has the
train tracks, roads with vehicles, and rivers with logs. I added the 2.5D to this game as well. It also has 
an added feature obstacle of a field of green with airplanes coming from the top, it's slow enough that the chicken 
has time to dodge it but as the user passes more obstacles, the airplane fields get harder.

The scoring box is on the top of the page labeled Score: starts off at 0 and as the chicken keeps passing and dodging 
obstacles and moving forward, it increases 1 for every step upward the chicken takes. If the chicken hits anything at any
point, it goes back to the start and the scoring resets to 0. 
There are the normal roads with vehicles that the chicken has to pass, there are train tracks that have a light that flashes red a second before a train appears, and there are river lanes that have logs passing by that the chicken has to hope from one to one to the grass to pass, if it touches the water, it restarts. The screen also moves downward like a normal crossy road but it's much slower. If the chicken gets to the top faster than the screen moves downward, the screen moves faster down so that the next obstacles are visible.    

I used ChatGPT to help with the code. I had a problem with my computer downloading Kiro so I wasn't able to use it. My overall strategy was being much slower with my words and engineering my prompts. I did it upgrade by upgrade, starting with the most basic one. Then I slowly added the train tracks, river lanes, then just some design issues and size issues I ran into, I fixed those, one by one. I also added the airplane and the fields upgrade. At the end, I switched it from 2D to 2.5D.
